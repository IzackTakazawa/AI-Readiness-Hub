Learning with AI

AI and Coursework

Use AI in ways that support the learning goal and match the rules of the assignment.

Level 0 — No AI

The assignment requires unaided work. Complete it without AI assistance.

Level 1 — AI Explore

AI may help you understand, question or explore, but the submitted work remains independently produced.

Level 2 — AI Integrated

AI is allowed as part of the workflow. You remain responsible for accuracy, explanation and disclosure.

Level 3 — AI Build

The assignment asks you to design, evaluate or build something using AI.

Before using AI on an assignment

Check three things: What is the learning goal? What has the instructor permitted? Can you personally explain, defend and verify what you submit?

  • Analyze images or experimental measurements
  • Detect patterns in environmental or sensor data
  • Compare AI predictions with established models

BEH

  • Compare human and AI coding of qualitative data
  • Explore patterns in survey responses
  • Analyze public policy/document corpora
  • Study human trust in AI
  • Investigate automation bias and decision making
NUR

Nursing & health

  • Analyze synthetic clinical datasets
  • Explore population-health trends
  • Compare AI suggestions with clinical reasoning
  • Investigate false positives and false negatives
  • Use approved de-identified research data

Humanities

  • Explore large collections of historical texts
  • Map themes or concepts across documents
  • Compare translations
  • Study discourse change over time
  • Use AI patterns to guide close reading

Safe & Responsible Use

Use AI in ways that protect your learning, your data and the integrity of your work.

Core rule: Never submit something you cannot explain, never trust an important claim you have not checked, and never place sensitive data into an AI system unless that use is explicitly approved.

Three checks before you use AI

Permission

Is AI use allowed for this class, assignment, workplace or research activity?

Privacy

Does your input contain personal, student, clinical, confidential or identifying information?

Verification

Can you independently check the important claims, calculations, sources or interpretations?
